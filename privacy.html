Weltrechner Upload (TikTok integration)

Weltrechner Upload uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @weltrechner to publish their own videos to that account. It processes: the access tokens the account owner grants (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish); basic profile data of that account (display name, avatar, open ID) and the posting options TikTok returns; and the owner's own videos, captions and hashtags.

No data of any other TikTok user is collected, stored, sold or shared. Tokens are kept only in the owner's private working environment and deleted when the connection is removed. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services. TikTok's own processing is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
